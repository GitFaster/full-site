SHAMEGRIN is the music project of Canadian artist Gary Makinson, creating dark emo rap, emotional rock, alternative hip hop and cinematic music shaped by raw personal storytelling, heartbreak, loneliness and late-night emotion.

Written at 3am

The SHAMEGRIN project is built around direct, confessional songwriting and atmosphere — songs that live between dark rap, emotional rock and cinematic alternative music.
