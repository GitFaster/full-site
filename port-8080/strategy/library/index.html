Public-domain classics

The Science of Getting Rich

Wallace D. Wattles, 1910. Full text: science-of-getting-rich.txt. Same book at Project Gutenberg 59844.

Key idea, in my words: Wattles argues you get rich by giving something people want more than the money they hand you, and by holding one definite aim instead of chasing every opening.

Acres of Diamonds

Russell H. Conwell. Full text: acres-of-diamonds.txt. Same book at Project Gutenberg 368.

Key idea, in my words: Conwell's lecture says the opportunity you are hunting is usually already in the work and the town you are standing in.
